Conclusion: The Leak That Moved Moderna Two Days Early

The data came out early, the selloff came early, and the leaked numbers were the real ones. Our wrap-up of the ESMO week.

Company Focus · 2 min read · 30 October 2026
Own market research
  1. 1Key points and conclusions by Surava Capital
  2. 2Written with AI assistance (Claude)
  3. 3Reviewed by Surava Capital

The Phase 3 data on Moderna's personalised melanoma vaccine arrived earlier than expected. The presentation was scheduled for Saturday, 24 October, at the ESMO Congress in Madrid (ESMO is the European Society for Medical Oncology). But on Thursday, 22 October, around 18:30 Zurich time, the results appeared on the medical news site Oncology Central. They were taken down shortly after, but screenshots spread quickly on X and Reddit.

The selloff therefore started two days earlier than planned, within the same trading session. The stock fell as much as 26% during the day and closed about 17% lower at 202.63 dollars. Without the leak, the market would only have reacted on Monday, 26 October.

The leaked figures turned out to be practically identical to the official results. ESMO lifted the embargo (the publication freeze) the same day, and Merck and Moderna confirmed the numbers: a 42% lower risk of the cancer returning or death compared with Keytruda alone, Merck's immunotherapy and today's standard treatment after surgery.

Who was behind the leak is still unclear, and ESMO is investigating. It is an interesting side story, but we won't pursue it further here. The next real test is the quarterly earnings report on 6 November.

Key takeaway

The leak only moved the timing, not the result. The data was good. The price drop was a classic “sell the news” reaction: investors sell as soon as long-awaited good news arrives, especially after a rally of over 500% since the start of the year. What matters now are the Q3 results on 6 November.
