How to read this. This is a simplified projection using a constant return every year. Real markets don't work that way, returns vary year to year, and the order of good and bad years matters (a downturn early in retirement is more damaging than the same downturn later, known as sequence-of-returns risk). Treat this as a way to build intuition about the relationship between spending, returns, and time, not as a prediction.
