How this journal works

  • Everything, not a selection. Every closed trade on the company in our focus goes in, losses included. Nothing gets deleted.
  • A fixed start. The record starts with our first trade on 21 October 2026.
  • Straight from the broker. Times and prices are copied from our Trading 212 export, not typed from memory.
  • Every change is dated and public. You can see each edit to this journal, with its date, in our public version history.
  • Percentages, not francs. We show the price move, not account size or profit in CHF.
