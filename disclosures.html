Disclosures

Important information about Surava Capital and the content published on this site.

Regulatory Status

Surava Capital is an independent financial education and market-commentary venture. It is not entered in the Swiss adviser register (Beraterregister) under FIDLEG and does not hold a licence under FINIG. We do not currently provide personal investment advice, portfolio management, or any other regulated financial service. Any services described as "planned" or "indicative" are not currently on offer.

Fee-Only Model

References to "fee-only" describe our intended future compensation structure, in which our only source of compensation would be fees paid directly by the people we work with, rather than commissions or other third-party payments. This describes an intention, not a current offering. Any duties we would owe in future depend on our registration status and applicable Swiss law at that time.

Risk of Loss

All investments involve risk, including the possible loss of principal invested. Diversification and asset allocation do not ensure a profit or guarantee against loss. Past performance is not indicative of future results, and there is no guarantee that any investment strategy will achieve its objectives.

Educational Content

Articles, guides, calculators, and tools available through the Education Hub are provided for general informational purposes only and do not take into account the specific objectives, financial situation, or needs of any individual. They should not be construed as personalized investment, legal, or tax advice.

Hypothetical Figures

Any fee figures, account minimums, or plan descriptions shown on this Site are indicative and illustrate our intended future model. They are not offers, and they do not represent actual results. Market calls published in the Insights and Trades sections are our own published views, documented in public, and are not recommendations to anyone.

Regulatory Information

Should Surava Capital enter the Swiss adviser register under FIDLEG in future, the disclosure documents, conduct rules, and ombudsman affiliation required at that point will be published here. Until then, no regulated financial service is being offered.

Questions

For questions about these disclosures, please contact us.
